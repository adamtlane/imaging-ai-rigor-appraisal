## Observations Beyond the 13 Items

1. **Clarity on Primary vs. Secondary Outcomes:** The study's prespecified primary endpoints (new AF diagnosis, NOAC prescription, echocardiogram arrangement, cardiologist visits) are predominantly behavioral and diagnostic process measures. While hard patient outcome measures (all-cause mortality, cardiovascular death, ischemic stroke) were evaluated as secondary endpoints, they did not reach statistical significance.


2. **Cluster Randomization Unit Disconnect:** While physicians (clusters) were randomized 1:1 to intervention or control, the primary outcome analysis evaluated patient-level events (n=275 vs n=245) using mixed-effects Cox proportional hazards modeling with physicians as a random factor.
