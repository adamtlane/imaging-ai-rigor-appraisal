## Observations

1. **Multi-Task & Multi-Dataset Structure:** RETFound is evaluated across 8 diagnostic datasets (e.g., APTOS-2019, IDRID, MESSIDOR-2), 1 prognosis task (fellow eye conversion to wet-AMD), and 4 systemic prediction tasks (stroke, MI, heart failure, Parkinson's). Across all these tasks, model performance metrics ($AUROC$ / $AUPR$) on retrospective datasets serve as the primary metrics (Tier 1 for Item 9).


2. **Close Verdicts / Alternative Consideration:** Item 6 was considered for `Unclear` due to the inclusion of RELPROP attention/relevance heatmaps. However, per the decision rules in the rating instructions ("Unclear if partial, for example saliency maps with no quantitative importance" and "No if the model is described only as an architecture"), `No` is the precise verdict required because vision transformers operate as black-box representation learners without explicit predictor importance lists or weights.
