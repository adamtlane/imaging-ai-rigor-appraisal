## Observations

* **Study Design Classification:** The study describes itself as a "retrospective pragmatic trial with historical and concurrent control subjects". Because allocation was based on geography/time (installation at the largest unit vs 5 control units) rather than randomization of individual patients or clusters, it is a non-randomized quasi-experimental implementation study. Class B rules applied accordingly.


* **Primary Outcome Tiering:** The study explicitly acknowledges the distinction between surrogate detection rates and true clinical outcomes in the discussion, noting on page 4: *"Whether AI will reduce postcolonoscopy colorectal cancer and mortality is a critical question."* Under the decision rules in `DEFINITIONS.md`, intent and candour do not alter the score; adenoma detection rate (ADR) and adenomas per colonoscopy (APC) are Tier 3 surrogates, resulting in `No` for Item 9.
