## Observations

1. **Intention-to-Screen Terminology vs. ITT:** Although the paper refers to an "intent-to-screen (ITS) study design" (p. 2), this reflects a single-arm diagnostic evaluation framework rather than a comparative trial with randomised arms. As such, Items 2, 3, 4, 11, and 12 correctly invoke Class B `N/A` exclusions per the rating instructions.


2. **Close Verdict Note on Item 9:** Item 9 was unequivocally assigned Tier 1. Although the paper discusses the downstream goal of preventing visual loss ("with the potential to help prevent vision loss in thousands of people with diabetes annually", p. 1), the operational definitions explicitly state that intent does not count and predicting/detecting disease remains Tier 1 / Tier 3. Sensitivity and specificity are model performance metrics (Tier 1), resulting in a definitive `No`.
