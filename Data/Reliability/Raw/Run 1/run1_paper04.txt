## Observations

* **Boundary Case on Item 11 (Intention-to-Treat):** Although the paper uses the term "modified intention to treat (mITT)", the analysis excludes 132 randomized patients post-randomization due to procedural quality, equipment, or protocol deviations (analyzing 841/973 randomized patients). Under the strict instruction rules, post-randomization exclusions force a verdict of `No` regardless of terminology.


* **Item 9 Tier Distinction:** The primary endpoint (Adenomas Per Colonoscopy, APC) measures mucosal lesion detection. As defined in `DEFINITIONS.md`, diagnostic and lesion detection counts belong strictly to Tier 3 (Surrogate) rather than Tier 4 (Patient Outcome) because detecting a lesion alters diagnostic knowledge rather than directly altering what the patient experiences without being told.
