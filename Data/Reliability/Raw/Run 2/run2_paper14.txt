## Observations

1. **Cluster Randomisation vs. Item 2 Strict Rule:** Item 2 mandates `No` for cluster randomized designs, even though physician-level cluster allocation is methodologically the appropriate design to prevent cross-contamination of physician decision-making in a clinical decision support system (CDSS) trial.


2. **Item 9 Tier Distinction:** Although secondary endpoints included genuine Tier 4 patient outcomes (ischemic stroke, cardiovascular death, all-cause death), the primary endpoints were process measures and intermediate clinical diagnoses (NOAC prescriptions, AF diagnosis, echocardiogram arrangement, cardiologist visits). Under `DEFINITIONS.md`, the primary endpoint tier governs, yielding a `No` verdict for Item 9.
