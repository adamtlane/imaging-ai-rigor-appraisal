## Additional Observations

* **Design Classification & Item Class Rules:** Although this paper uses the word "trial" in its title ("Computer-aided Detection of Polyps Does Not Improve Colonoscopist Performance in a Pragmatic Implementation Trial"), it is explicitly a retrospective quasi-experimental difference-in-differences implementation study using historical and concurrent site controls without participant or cluster randomisation. Under the provided rater instructions, Class B items (Items 3, 4, 5, 11, and 12) permit `N/A` when a paper has no trial arms. Class B items 3, 4, 5, and 11 were marked `N/A` on this basis. Item 12 was marked `Yes` because baseline comparison tables across study periods and control sites were provided (Supplementary Table 1).
