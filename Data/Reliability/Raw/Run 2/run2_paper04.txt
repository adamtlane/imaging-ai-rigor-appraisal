## Observations

* **Endpoint / Impact Distinction:** While the trial is a well-conducted, patient-level parallel-group RCT evaluating a novel cloud-based AI system in real time, it demonstrates clinical impact as an intervention study measuring endoscopic performance (Tier 3 surrogate detection metrics) rather than direct long-term patient health outcomes (Tier 4, e.g., post-colonoscopy colorectal cancer incidence or mortality).
