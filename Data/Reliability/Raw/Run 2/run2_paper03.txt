## Observations

* **Study Paradigm:** Although the study employs an "intent-to-screen" cohort paradigm and was registered on ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT02963441) as a pivotal registration trial for FDA authorization, it functions as a single-arm prospective diagnostic validation study rather than a comparative randomized controlled trial. Consequently, items requiring randomized arms (3, 4, 11, 12) or comparative impact evaluations (7) score as `N/A` under Class B and Class C guidelines.
