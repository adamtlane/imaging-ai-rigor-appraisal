## Observations Beyond the Checklist

* **Sample Size / Baseline Discrepancy:** The trial's power calculation assumed a control arm baseline APC of $0.83$. In the actual study, the control arm achieved an APC of $0.62 \pm 1.19$. Because the baseline event rate was materially lower than assumed, the statistical power assumed *a priori* was altered in practice, triggering a `No` on Item 10 under the specified decision rules.


* **Excluded Post-Randomization Cases:** A total of 973 participants were randomized, but 132 patients ($13.6\%$) were excluded post-randomization across multiple stages (e.g., inadequate bowel prep, withdrawal time $<6$ minutes, unapproved scope models). Analysis was performed on a modified intention-to-treat / per-protocol cohort ($n=841$), which fails the strict full ITT criterion for Item 11.
