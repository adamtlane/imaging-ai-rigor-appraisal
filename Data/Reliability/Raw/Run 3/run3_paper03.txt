## Additional Observations

1. **Class B Items (Items 3, 4, 5, 11, 12):** Although this paper includes prospective registration on ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT02963441), pre-specified endpoints, and detailed power calculations, it is a single-arm diagnostic accuracy evaluation without comparative trial arms. Per the rules governing Class B items in `RATER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, all trial-arm specific items score `N/A`.


2. **Intent-to-Screen Analysis vs. ITT:** The study authors explicitly invoke an "intent-to-screen (ITS)" framework. However, 73 of the 892 ITS participants (8.2%) were excluded from primary statistical analysis due to ungradable FPRC images or AI system image quality issues. If evaluated under trial standards, these post-enrollment exclusions would fail ITT criteria, but `N/A` applies here due to Class B permitting rules for non-arm observational designs.
