## Observations

* **Cluster Randomisation vs. Patient Outcome Identification:** While the trial evaluates an AI decision support tool allocated at the physician level (cluster RCT), secondary clinical outcomes such as ischemic stroke, cardiovascular death, and all-cause death were evaluated. However, because Item 9 strictly evaluates the primary endpoints (prescribing rates and diagnosis rates, which sit at Tiers 2 and 3), the paper receives `No` for Item 9 per the operational definitions.


* **Baseline Rate Deviation in Sample Size:** The trial assumed a baseline control prescription rate of 17.1% (based on prior literature), but observed a control rate of 12.0%. Because both arms successfully met and exceeded the prespecified sample size requirement of 241 patients per group (275 vs. 245), Item 10 is scored `Yes`.
